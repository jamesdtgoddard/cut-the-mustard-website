FAQ

Good questions.

Can't see what you're looking for? Drop us a message.

Where do you travel?

Anywhere in the UK. Travel costs are included in your quote, so there are no surprises.

Can we choose the songs?

Absolutely. Send us your must-plays and do-not-plays and we'll build the sets around you. We'll also learn a special song for your first dance.

How much space do you need?

Around 4m × 3m is ideal, plus access to a couple of standard power sockets nearby. We can adapt to most venues, so just ask.

Does the venue have a sound limiter?

Lots do! We're used to working with limiters and can adjust our setup so the party keeps going without cutting out.

Are you insured?

Yes. We hold public liability insurance and all our equipment is PAT tested. We're happy to send certificates to your venue.

How far in advance should we book?

Summer Saturdays get snapped up 12 to 18 months ahead, so the sooner the better. That said, it's always worth asking about last-minute dates.
